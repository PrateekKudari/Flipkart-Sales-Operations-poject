# Flipkart Sales & Operations Dashboard

An interactive e-commerce analytics dashboard designed to explore sales performance, order trends, delivery operations, customer ratings, returns, and cancellations.

## Project Overview

The **Flipkart Sales & Operations Dashboard** provides an interactive view of e-commerce business performance through visual analytics and key performance indicators (KPIs).

The dashboard helps users understand sales trends, compare regional and category performance, monitor delivery efficiency, and identify operational areas that may need improvement.

It includes three interactive sections — **Overview, Operations, and Details** — to explore business performance from different perspectives.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and order performance.
- Identify monthly revenue trends.
- Compare sales across product categories and regions.
- Analyze payment method distribution.
- Evaluate delivery performance across city tiers.
- Track product returns and order cancellations.
- Explore detailed business data through interactive filters and tables.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Overview

- Key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Monthly net sales trends.
- Orders by payment method.
- Net sales by product category.
- Net sales by region.

### 2. Operations

- On-time delivery performance by city tier.
- Average delivery time by city tier.
- Customer ratings comparison: late vs. on-time deliveries.
- Return rate by category.
- Cancellation rate by region.

### 3. Details

- Interactive data table.
- Grouping by Category, Region, and City Tier.
- Sorting and filtering capabilities.
- CSV download option.
- Automatically generated key insights.

### 4. Interactive Controls

- Year filter: 2024, 2025, and 2026.
- Region and category filters.
- Reset filters option.
- Multiple dashboard themes:
  - Classic
  - Big Billion Days
  - Plus Dark

## Key Business Questions

This dashboard is designed to help answer questions such as:

1. How does net sales performance change month over month?
2. Which product categories contribute the most to net sales?
3. Which regions generate higher sales?
4. Which payment methods are most commonly used?
5. Which city tiers have better on-time delivery performance?
6. Does late delivery have a relationship with customer ratings?
7. Which categories have higher return rates?
8. Which regions have higher cancellation rates?

## Dataset Disclaimer

**Important:** This project uses synthetic data generated to simulate e-commerce business patterns. It is not official Flipkart data and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Flipkart.

The dashboard covers 2024, 2025, and 2026. The 2026 data covers January to October. Year-over-year comparisons for 2026 use the corresponding months of 2025.
